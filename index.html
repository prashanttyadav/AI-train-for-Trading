How to Play

  • Infected villager select karo (purple ring).
  • Green highlighted villagers par spread click karo.
  • Guard red radius mein source/target hone par spread fail hoga.
  • Har move ke baad guards patrol shift karte hain.
  • Turn limit ke andar sabko infect karo.
